# Video 022 — Visual Clarity Checklist (pre-generation)

Reference: user-provided 8-panel comic, "My toaster has a tiny bread boxing
match inside." Replaces the prior cinematic-silhouette version of 022, which
was judged too abstract — the boxing action did not read clearly even with
subtitles. This checklist must be visually verified against actual Kling
output before any QC pass or upload is considered.

## Must be true on sight, subtitles off

- [ ] Viewer can say "bread slices are boxing inside a toaster" within the
      first 2 scenes, without reading text.
- [ ] Toaster is recognizable as an ordinary, unbranded toaster in scene 1.
- [ ] Boxing ring (ropes + corner posts) is unmistakable in scene 2, not an
      abstract glowing shape.
- [ ] Two bread-slice boxer characters are clearly distinguished from each
      other: wheat/white bread = light color + red gloves; dark rye bread =
      dark color + blue gloves. Color and glove-color pairing must stay
      consistent across scenes 3, 4, and 5.
- [ ] Both bread characters have visible, readable faces (eyes, eyebrows,
      mouth/expression) — not silhouettes.
- [ ] Boxing gloves are clearly rendered on both characters, not abstract
      blobs.
- [ ] Crumb crowd is visible and reads as "a crowd," not background texture.
- [ ] The punch in scene 4 is a single readable action (clear glove contact
      + clear reaction), not motion blur or an ambiguous gesture.
- [ ] The fallen bread character in scene 4 is clearly down/reacting, not
      just another shot of static bread.
- [ ] Winning character in scene 5 is clearly wearing the championship belt
      and gloves, popping out of the toaster in a readable, well-lit shot.
- [ ] No scene relies on shadow/silhouette to obscure unclear character
      shapes — lighting must be even enough that nothing is hidden.

## Hard rules (unchanged, still enforced)

- [ ] No real human face, no celebrity, no copyrighted character.
- [ ] No brand logos or readable packaging text anywhere (toaster, props).
- [ ] No blood, gore, or realistic violence — punch and fall stay playful/
      surreal, not injurious.
- [ ] Does not look like a cheap kids' cartoon (per channel direction), but
      is allowed to be more overtly funny/expressive than prior "cinematic
      silhouette" videos — clarity takes priority over moodiness here.

## Process note

This checklist is to be re-run as a manual frame-by-frame visual check
(same method used for the prior retention edit: extract frames at hook /
reveal / fighters / punch / final, inspect each) immediately after live
Kling generation, before QC and before any upload decision.
