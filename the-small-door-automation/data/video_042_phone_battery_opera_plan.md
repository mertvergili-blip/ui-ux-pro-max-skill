## 1. FINAL HOOK

**Title:** My phone battery doesn't die. It performs.

**Opening line (caption / voice):**
"My phone hit 1%."
"It didn't ask to be charged."
"It asked for an audience."

**Why this hook works:**
- 1% battery panic = universal. Every human has felt this.
- "It performs" is the WTF flip. Instantly reframes a mundane moment.
- No setup required. Viewer understands the premise in frame 1.

---

## 2. STORY FLOW — 25–28 SECONDS

```
0–3s    HOOK      Phone screen shows 1%. Camera pushes through screen.
3–8s    REVEAL    Opera stage inside the phone. Battery performing death scene.
8–14s   BUILD     Battery's monologue. Charger runs in from backstage. Ignored.
14–19s  ESCALATE  Charger points to outlet 3 feet away. Battery refuses to stop.
19–24s  PUNCHLINE Battery collapses. Final line. Phone dies.
24–28s  BUTTON    Phone screen goes black. One beat. Low battery chime plays.
```

Core emotional arc:
Viewer laughs at battery's dramatics → empathizes with charger's panic →
punchline lands because the "solution" was always obvious but ignored.

## 3. THREE SCRIPT VERSIONS

---

### VERSION A — CLEAN / BROAD (safest, widest audience)

**Battery (dying dramatically):**
"The signal is fading... the apps are going dark..."
"Tell the WiFi... I tried."

**Charger (panicking, running in):**
"YOU'RE ONE METER FROM THE OUTLET."

**Battery (ignoring charger, final monologue):**
"Let me finish."

**Battery (collapses, whispers):**
"...low battery."

**Caption cue (end):**
"Should I plug it in\nor let it finish?"

**Energy:** Classic dry absurdist. Clean enough for any audience.
**Weakness:** Slightly safe. Punchline predictable.

---

### VERSION B — EDGY / ADULT-ANIMATION (recommended)

**Battery (dying dramatically, mid-aria):**
"I gave everything. Every percent. Every bar."
"Tell the WiFi I loved it more than it ever loved me."

**Charger (running in, losing it):**
"IT'S THREE FEET. THREE. FEET."

**Battery (without opening eyes):**
"I'm in the middle of something."

**Charger:**
"You're at ONE PERCENT. You're not dying, you're being dramatic."

**Battery:**
"Those are the same thing."

**Battery (collapses with full theatrical commitment):**
"...tell Spotify... my playlist was never finished."

**Phone dies. Screen goes black.**

**Caption cue (end):**
"Should I charge it\nor let it have this?"

**Energy:** Adult-animation tone. Sharp, self-aware, rewatch value high.
**Why this works:** The "those are the same thing" line is the real punchline.
Charger's frustration mirrors the viewer's. Battery's logic is absurd but internally consistent.

---

### VERSION C — EDGY WITH EDGE (censored in captions)

**Battery:**
"I gave every damn percent I had."
"Nobody appreciated a single bar."

**Charger:**
"You're literally next to the outlet, what the f*** are you doing."

**Battery (calm, not opening eyes):**
"I'm performing."

**Charger:**
"YOU'RE AT ONE PERCENT."

**Battery:**
"Then let me die with dignity."

**Charger:**
"You don't have dignity. You have a USB port."

**Battery (final, collapsing):**
"...at least I didn't charge for TikTok."

**Caption cue (end):**
"Should I plug it in\nor does it deserve this?"

**Energy:** Sharper edges, more confrontational humor.
**Captions:** "damn" stays, "f***" censored in caption.
**Risk:** Narrower audience, but higher share-for-shock value.

---

## 4. RECOMMENDED VERSION: VERSION B

**Reason:**
- Sharp but not offensive — no actual profanity in audio
- "Those are the same thing" = the cleanest punchline of all three
- Charger frustration = viewer surrogate = emotional connection
- "tell Spotify my playlist was never finished" = specific + relatable + funny
- Rewatch value: people will replay for the delivery
- Caption-safe: no censoring needed, all text clean
- Comment bait: "Should I charge it or let it have this?" = 100% comment-driving

## 5. FIVE-SCENE PLAN

### Scene 01 — HOOK (3s)
**Action:** Phone sits on a table, screen glowing. 1% battery icon in corner. Camera slowly pushes into the screen.
**Karakter:** Dış telefon — unbranded, generic black rectangle
**Camera:** Slow push-in through screen. Transition: screen becomes a stage curtain.
**Caption:** "My phone hit 1%."
**Audio cue:** Phone ambient hum + low single piano note
**Visual risk:** Screen must be generic — no Apple/Samsung logo, no real app icons visible externally. Battery icon in corner must read as "1%" without showing real iOS/Android UI.

---

### Scene 02 — REVEAL (5s)
**Action:** Red velvet curtain parts. Small opera stage inside the phone. CENTER STAGE: Battery character in spotlight — white/pale, opera cape, hand to forehead, arm reaching up. Tiny generic app-icon silhouette audience visible in the background. Stage floor of wooden planks. Everything clearly lit.
**Karakter:** Battery — pale/faded yellow-white, opera cape, theatrical pose, spotlight center
**Camera:** Wide establishing → slow push to medium on battery character
**Caption:** "It wasn't dying.\nIt was performing."
**Audio cue:** Dramatic operatic swell (tiny/cartoonish version) + audience murmur
**Visual risk:** App icon seyirciler generic olmalı — hiçbir gerçek logo/ikon yok. Battery karakterinin yüzü tam net olmalı bu sahnede.

---

### Scene 03 — BUILD / MONOLOGUE (6s)
**Action:** Battery performing its death monologue. Full center frame. One hand to forehead (acı çeken), other hand reaching toward spotlight above. Expression: kaşlar derin V üzgün, ağız açık aria-style. BACKSTAGE'DEN Charger cable koşarak giriyor — turuncu, gözler fırlamış, arms outstretched, panik.
**Karakter:** Battery (center, large) + Charger (entering from stage right, smaller)
**Camera:** Medium shot — both in frame but battery dominates
**Caption:** "I gave every percent I had.\nTell the WiFi I tried."
**Audio cue:** Battery voice (tiny operatic tenor, cartoonish) + Charger's footsteps/panicked entrance
**Visual risk:** İki karakterin rengi tam zıt olmalı: soluk beyaz/sarı battery vs parlak turuncu charger. Battery büyük ve merkezi, charger daha küçük ve kenarda bu sahnede.

---

### Scene 04 — ESCALATE / CONFRONTATION (7s)
**Action:** Charger tries to grab Battery arm. Battery pulls away without breaking performance. CHARGER points off-stage left (toward "the outlet") — exaggerated pointing gesture, fury on face, veins showing. BATTERY, without opening eyes: "I'm in the middle of something." Charger: "YOU'RE AT ONE PERCENT." Battery, still performing: "Those are the same thing." Charger holds head with both hands in disbelief.
**Karakter:** Both characters medium shot — confrontation framing
**Camera:** Medium two-shot. Slight push-in on the key exchange.
**Caption:** "\"I'm in the middle\nof something.\"\n\"Those are the same thing.\""
**Audio cue:** Sharp voice exchange — battery calm/theatrical, charger strained/panicked. Tiny audience gasps.
**Visual risk:** Bu sahne en fazla konuşma içeren sahne. Konuşan karakterin yüzü her replikte net görünmeli. Charger'ın işaret parmağı / pointing gesture net okunmalı.

---

### Scene 05 — PUNCHLINE + BUTTON (7s)
**Action:** Battery begins final collapse — slow theatrical fall, one arm still outstretched, cape billowing, spotlight tightens on it. Final whisper: "Tell Spotify... my playlist was never finished." Charger catches battery (or tries to) — arms out, horrified expression. PHONE GOES DARK. Single low-battery chime. One beat of silence. Caption appears: "Should I charge it / or let it have this?"
**Karakter:** Battery collapsing center stage, charger reacting
**Camera:** Starts medium → slow pull-back as battery falls → full stage dark
**Caption:** "Tell Spotify...\nmy playlist was\nnever finished.\"\n\nShould I charge it\nor let it have this?"
**Audio cue:** Opera swell peaks then cuts. Charger's panicked cry. Then: single low-battery chime in silence.
**Visual risk:** Collapse must read as theatrical/comedic, not sad. Cape billowing = cinematic but clear. Dark ending must not obscure the characters — transition to black should be rapid and clean, not a gradual fade that hides the punchline frame.

## 7. VOICE DIRECTION

### Battery character voice
- Tiny operatic tenor — but cartoonish, slightly squeaky
- Absolute commitment to the drama — no irony in the delivery
- Deliberate, slow, each word a performance
- B-cartoon post-process chain: slight pitch up + reverb (theater hall) + slight rasp on held notes
- Think: tiny Pavarotti who hasn't eaten

### Charger cable voice
- Fast, strained, losing patience, near-cracking
- The voice of someone who has watched this happen 47 times
- No reverb — dry, immediate, present
- B-cartoon post-process: slight pitch variation (goes higher as panic increases)
- Think: exhausted stage manager who should have quit

### Spotify line delivery
- Battery whispers it — barely audible but clear
- This is the laugh-after-the-laugh line — needs space before and after
- Single note piano hits after it, then silence

## 10. PRODUCTION RISK ANALYSIS

### Risks from 022 — What We Learned, How 042 Fixes It

| 022 Problem | 042 Solution |
|-------------|--------------|
| İki karakter aynı tonlarda çıktı (renk karışması) | Battery = pale white/yellow, Charger = bright orange. Impossible to confuse. |
| Punch action motion blur içinde kayboldu | Scene 04'te punching yok — confrontation is static/gesturing. Cleaner action. |
| Winner sahnesi uzak planla geldi, net okumadı | Scene 05 collapse = close/medium shot. Battery fills 60% of frame. |
| Scene 3 trash-talk ve scene 4 KO visually mismatched | All 5 scenes share same world, same characters, same lighting. No mismatch risk. |
| Referans görselsiz ilk 3 sahne farklı style'da çıktı | Tüm sahneler aynı karakter tanımını kullanıyor. Image2video veya çok detaylı prompt ile karakterler kilitlenir. |
| Crowd sahneyi boğdu bazı karelerde | App icon seyirciler explicitly "background, small, dark" olarak prompt'a yazılacak. |

### Remaining Risks

**Risk 1 — Battery'nin "transparent/ethereal" görünmesi**
- Soluk/faded pil ikonu ile "ölmekte olan şeffaf karakter" arasında ince çizgi var.
- Fix: prompt'a "solid opaque pale yellow/white, NOT transparent, NOT glowing orb" yaz.

**Risk 2 — Opera cape okunmaması**
- Cape küçük karakterde gözden kaçabilir.
- Fix: cape kırmızı ve büyük tanımlanmalı. "large flowing red opera cape, clearly visible, billowing behind the character"

**Risk 3 — Charger cable'ın "just a cable" görünmesi (yüzsüz)**
- Kablo anthropomorphic değil çıkarsa sahne okunmaz.
- Fix: "orange charger cable character with a round face on the plug end, large panicking eyes, arms extended from the cable body"

**Risk 4 — Sahne 05 karanlığa geçerken karakterlerin kaybolması**
- Önceki videolarda çok karanlık sahneler yüzler gizledi.
- Fix: "lights fade AFTER the collapse is fully visible — do not fade during the action, only after battery is clearly on the stage floor"

**Risk 5 — App icon seyirciler tanınabilir ikon olarak çıkması**
- Generic olması lazım ama model gerçek app iconlarına çekebilir.
- Fix: "abstract colored square audience characters, no real app logos, no text, no recognizable brand icons"

### Production Difficulty: Easy-Medium
- 2 ana karakter (en az sahne karmaşıklığı)
- Tek sabit dünya (opera sahne — her sahnede aynı)
- Aksiyon kavga değil diyalog/jest — motion blur riski düşük
- Işık: spotlight dominant — basit ve güçlü
