## Bleep/censor rules

Two lines require censoring: line 4 ("I'll [bleep] you up.") and line 7
("Breakfast is over, [bleep]."). Both follow the same rule:

- **Audio**: the literal swear word is never sent to or generated by the TTS
  engine. `generate_voice_022.py` sends ElevenLabs a literal "beep"
  placeholder in that word's position (`"I'll beep you up."`,
  `"Breakfast is over, beep."`). The eventual mix step
  (`mix_voice_and_sfx_022.py`) overlays a short censor-beep/tone over that
  word's timing — no uncensored profanity is ever spoken or rendered.
- **Caption**: rendered censored on screen (`f***`, `b****`) — never spelled
  out in full, never ahead of or contradicting the audio beep.

final mix — raw ElevenLabs output is never used unprocessed in the final
video.
