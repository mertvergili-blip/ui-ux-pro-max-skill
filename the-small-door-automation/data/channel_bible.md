# The Small Door — Channel Bible

## Identity

The Small Door is a faceless, global YouTube Shorts channel revealing tiny,
magical worlds hidden behind, inside, or under ordinary objects.

**Tagline:** Tiny hidden worlds behind ordinary things.

**Language:** English only. No narrator, no long voice-over. Only short
subtitles, ASMR sound, and ambience.

## Tone & Atmosphere

- Cinematic, cozy, mysterious, magical realism.
- Dark but warmly lit: rainy nights, dim rooms, soft shadows, warm window light.
- Premium and consistent — never a generic "AI spam" channel.
- Not a kids' channel: no oversaturated cartoon look, no childish colors.

## Hard Bans

- No human faces, no real people, no celebrities.
- No brand names, logos, or readable brand text.
- No copyrighted characters.
- No narrator voice-over.
- No horror, gore, or violence.
- No toy-like / plastic / oversaturated cartoon visuals.

## Visual World

Tiny doors, miniature streets, dim lights, old books, drawers, mirrors, teacups,
refrigerators, glass bottles, clocks, fabric textures, fog, rain, candlelight,
old wooden surfaces, moss/greenery, warm tiny city lights.

## Core Feeling

Like the viewer's own home secretly contains tiny hidden worlds nobody else has
noticed. Every video should leave the viewer asking: "what's behind the door?"

## Video Format

- YouTube Shorts, 9:16 vertical, 25–35 seconds.
- Short English subtitles, ASMR sound design, no long voice-over.

## Five-Part Story Formula

1. **0–2s — Hook:** a strong curiosity statement. *"I opened the fridge at 3AM."*
2. **2–8s — Discovery:** an ordinary object or hidden door is found.
   *"Behind the milk, something was glowing."*
3. **8–25s — Reveal:** the miniature world appears.
   *"It was a tiny train station."*
4. **25–32s — Twist:** a small twist lands. *"The ticket had my name on it."*
5. **Final 1–2s — Question:** a comment-baiting question. *"Should I get on?"*

## Quality Standard

Every video must feel like a small cinematic micro-story, not a copy-pasted
template. Each video uses a different object, a different miniature world, a
different sound plan, and a different twist. No two videos should feel
interchangeable.

## Subseries

Fridge Worlds, Book Worlds, Mirror Worlds, Cup Worlds, Drawer Worlds,
Rainy Night Stories, Clock Worlds, Bottle Worlds, Tiny Shops, Hidden Transit,
Tiny Nature, Candle Worlds, Paper Worlds, Home Appliance Worlds, Box Worlds,
Small Door Core.

## Publishing Rule

The system never publishes publicly on its own. Every video is produced as
**private** on YouTube. A human reviews and approves before making it public.
