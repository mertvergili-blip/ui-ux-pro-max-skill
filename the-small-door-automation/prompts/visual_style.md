# Visual Style — The Small Door

Use this as the base visual style block for every scene prompt.

```
cinematic macro shot, miniature hidden world, cozy magical realism, dark room,
warm tiny lights, soft shadows, shallow depth of field, realistic textures,
no humans, no logos, no text, no brand packaging, no cartoon style
```

## Recurring motifs

tiny doors, miniature streets, dim lights, old books, drawers, mirrors,
teacups, refrigerators, glass bottles, clocks, fabric textures, fog, rain,
candlelight, old wooden surfaces, moss/greenery, warm tiny city lights.

## Lighting

Warm practical lights (candle, lantern, tiny windows) against a dark,
rainy-night-adjacent ambient room. High contrast, low key, soft falloff.

## Camera

Macro/close-up lens feel, slow push-ins, slight parallax, shallow depth of
field, occasional rack focus to reveal the hidden world.

## The Small Door Visual Clarity Rule (apply to every scene prompt)

One-time benchmark derived from reference images for video 022 — not to
be copied literally into future prompts, only the standard below applies:

- Main character(s) centered, large enough, and instantly readable —
  never lost in background detail.
- Objects acting human (face/eyes/mouth/arms/small accessories) are
  encouraged when on-brand and original.
- Action must be understandable at a glance, without relying on
  subtitles or sound.
- The miniature world/society around the character(s) must read clearly
  — avoid overly dark or abstract compositions.
- Clear WTF action takes priority over moody/cinematic ambiguity; keep
  the cinematic visual_style.md texture, but never let it obscure the
  subject or action.
- Leave clear, caption-safe space (center or safe margins) — don't place
  key action where burned-in captions will sit.
