# Visual Style — The Small Door

Use this as the base visual style block for every scene prompt.

```
cinematic macro shot, miniature hidden world, cozy magical realism, dark room,
warm tiny lights, soft shadows, shallow depth of field, realistic textures,
no humans, no logos, no text, no brand packaging, no cartoon style
```

## Recurring motifs

tiny doors, miniature streets, dim lights, old books, drawers, mirrors,
teacups, refrigerators, glass bottles, clocks, fabric textures, fog, rain,
candlelight, old wooden surfaces, moss/greenery, warm tiny city lights.

## Lighting

Warm practical lights (candle, lantern, tiny windows) against a dark,
rainy-night-adjacent ambient room. High contrast, low key, soft falloff.

## Camera

Macro/close-up lens feel, slow push-ins, slight parallax, shallow depth of
field, occasional rack focus to reveal the hidden world.
