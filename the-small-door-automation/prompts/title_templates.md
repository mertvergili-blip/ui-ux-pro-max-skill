# Title Templates

Target: 45–70 characters, curiosity-driven, not spammy, natural English.

- I found a tiny {world} inside my {object}
- A {world} opened inside my {object}
- There was a {world} inside my {object}
- I found {world} in my {object}
- A tiny {world} lived inside my {object}

## Examples

- I found a tiny train station inside my fridge
- A bakery opened inside my old book
- There was a glowing forest inside my coffee cup
